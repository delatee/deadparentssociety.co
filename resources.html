Resources

Things that might help

A curated list of support services, reading, and other resources for people navigating grief. They are recommended by our members and we regularly update this list with new materials.

If you need help right now

DPS is a peer support group, not a crisis service. If you're struggling and need to talk to someone urgently, please reach out to one of these services.

📞
Lifeline — 13 11 14 24/7 crisis support and suicide prevention. Call, chat online, or text 0477 13 11 14.
💙
Beyond Blue — 1300 22 4636 Support for anxiety, depression and related conditions. Available 24/7 by phone, chat, and email.
🧠
headspace Mental health support specifically for young people aged 12–25. Centres across Australia and online options.

Grief support services

These organisations specialise in bereavement and loss and are a good place to find 1-1 support and therapy.

🌿
Canberra Grief Centre Provides services to support grief, bereavement and trauma associated with a loss. In person support available in Canberra.
🌿
Grief Australia Free grief support services for Australians, including telephone support and referrals to local counsellors.
🌿
National Association for Loss and Grief (NALAG) Free grief support to individuals, groups and communities across Australia in response to loss, grief and trauma. In person support available in Dubbo, Mudgee and Hunter NSW.

Practical support

Sometimes grief intersects with practical matters that nobody prepares you for. These resources can help.

📄
Services Austrlia - what to do when someone dies Step-by-step guide of what to do and how to manage the adminsitration.
📄
MyGov - Payments and managing your money when someone dies Information about Centrelink bereavement payments and other financial support available to those who have lost a family member.
