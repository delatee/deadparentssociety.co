Start a group

Bring DPS to your city.

Every DPS chapter starts with one person who decides their city needs this. If that person is you, we'll back you the whole way.

Why start a chapter?

Grief is everywhere, but peer support for young adults who've lost a parent is still rare in most Australian cities. If you've experienced DPS — or just understand what it is — you already have everything you need to start one.

You don't need to be a trained counsellor. You don't need formal qualifications. You need to understand the experience, be willing to hold space for others, and be organised enough to get people in a room once a month.

Important: DPS chapters are peer support groups, not clinical services. If you're running a chapter, you're facilitating conversation — not providing therapy. Please be clear about this distinction in all your communications.

How to get started

1

Get in touch with us

Email us at hello@deadparentssociety.co to let us know you're interested. We'll have a conversation, share our facilitation resources, and make sure you feel supported before your first session.

2

Find a venue

You don't need anything fancy. A quiet room at a library, community centre, church hall, or pub function room all work well. Aim for something accessible, free or low-cost, and comfortable — chairs in a circle, no boardroom tables. Many venues will offer free use of space for community support groups.

3

Spread the word

Start local. Post in Facebook community groups, Reddit city forums, university notice boards, and local Instagram pages. A simple flyer works well. Word of mouth is your most powerful tool — even one or two people in the first session is a success.

4

Run your first session

We'll provide a simple facilitation guide so you're not starting from scratch. The structure is gentle: welcome, introductions, themed conversation, open sharing, close. Keep it loose, keep it human.

5

Keep going

The first session is always the hardest. By the third, you'll have regulars. By the sixth, people will be telling others. Consistency is the most important ingredient — show up every month, even when turnout is low.

What we'll give you

When you start a DPS chapter, you're not on your own. Here's what we provide to every new chapter lead:

📋

Facilitation guide

A simple, practical guide to running sessions — including how to open, how to handle difficult moments, and how to close well.

🗓️

Session plans

A library of themed session plans you can use or adapt — so you don't have to invent a new topic every month.

🖨️

Promotional materials

Flyer templates, social media graphics, and suggested copy to help you get the word out.

💬

Ongoing support

A direct line to the Canberra team. We'll check in, answer questions, and debrief with you when you need it.

Ready to start?

The first step is just sending us an email. We'd love to hear from you.
