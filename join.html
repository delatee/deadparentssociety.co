How to join

You're already welcome.

There's no application process, no waiting list, and no membership fee. If you've lost a parent and you're roughly in your 20s, you belong here.

Getting started

Joining DPS is simple. Here's all you need to do:

1

Check the session calendar

Head to the Sessions page to find the next gathering near you. Sessions run monthly in Canberra, with more cities coming.

2

Let us know you're coming (optional)

You don't have to, but it helps us prepare the space. Drop us an email at hello@deadparentssociety.co or send us a message on Instagram or Facebook.

3

Show up

That's it. Come as you are. Bring a friend if it helps, or come alone — lots of people do. Doors open 15 minutes before the session starts.

Our group agreements

DPS works because everyone in the room agrees to a few simple things. These aren't rules — they're the conditions that make honest conversation possible.

🤐

Confidentiality

What's shared in the room stays in the room. You can talk about your own experience outside DPS, but not anyone else's.

👂

Listen to understand

When someone is sharing, our job is to listen — not to fix, advise, or relate it back to our own experience. There's time for that later.

🧭

Speak for yourself

Use "I" statements. Your experience is valid; we're not here to speak for others or tell them how they should feel.

🚪

You can step out

If something gets overwhelming, it's completely fine to step outside for a moment. You don't need permission.

Stay in the loop

The best way to stay up to date with sessions, new cities, and what we're up to is to follow us on social media or subscribe to our mailing list.

📬 Email updates: Send us an email at hello@deadparentssociety.co with "Add me to the list" and we'll keep you posted on upcoming sessions.

You can also find us on Instagram and Facebook where we share session reminders and occasional reflections from our community.

Ready?

The hardest part is usually just walking in the door. We promise it gets easier after that.
