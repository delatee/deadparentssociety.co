What actually happens

Each session has a loose structure. Here's roughly what to expect:

1

Arrive and settle in

We'll be there a few minutes before the start time. You can buy a tea, coffee or other beverage at the bar. You don't need to know anyone, in fact most people don't when they first join.

2

Welcome and introductions

The facilitator opens the session with a brief welcome and a reminder of our group agreements. Everyone shares their first name and, if they want to, a word or two about who they lost.

3

Themed conversation

Each session has a loose theme, for example coping strategies, surprises in grief and nagivating relationships. The theme is a starting point, not a constraint - our conversations go where they need to go for the group there on the day.

4

Open sharing

Sharing is optional and encouraged if you are ready to. It's absolutely fine to just listen.
