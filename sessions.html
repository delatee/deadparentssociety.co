What actually happens

Each session has a loose structure, but nothing rigid. Here's roughly what to expect:

1

Arrive and settle in

Doors open 15 minutes before the session. There's usually tea, coffee, and something to eat. You don't need to know anyone — most people arrive not knowing anyone.

2

Welcome and introductions

A facilitator opens the session with a brief welcome and a reminder of our group agreements — confidentiality, respect, no fixing. Everyone shares their first name and, if they want to, a word or two about who they lost.

3

Themed conversation

Each session has a loose theme — something like anniversaries and difficult dates, or what grief looks like at work. The theme is a starting point, not a constraint. Conversations go where they need to go.

4

Open sharing

The floor opens for anything on people's minds. Nobody has to share. There's no pressure, no going around the circle unless people want that.

5

Close

We close gently, usually with a short check-out. People often stay and chat afterwards — this informal time is often just as valuable as the session itself.
